About The Show

Live From The Back Deck

A Different Kind Of Show

Back Deck Live is the relaxed, conversational side of CrowRules Entertainment. It is where the conversation can go somewhere unexpected.

The show connects Tacoma, the Pacific Northwest, sports, entertainment, gaming, personal stories, and the larger CrowRules universe.

The Companion To Tacoma Nights

Tacoma Nights is the structured weekly show. Back Deck Live is where the conversation gets looser.

Together, the two shows form an important part of the CrowRules Entertainment original programming lineup.
