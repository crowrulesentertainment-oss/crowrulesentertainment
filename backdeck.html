About The Show

Live From The Back Deck

WHAT IS BACK DECK LIVE?

Back Deck Live is the weekly live companion to Tacoma Nights — a relaxed, conversational broadcast built around Tacoma, the Pacific Northwest and the world of CrowRules Entertainment.

Sometimes there's a plan. Sometimes there's a story. Sometimes you just hit Go Live and see what happens.

WHY THE BACK DECK?

The Back Deck is intentionally different from a traditional studio. It is informal, personal and connected to everyday life.

That makes it the perfect place for conversations that don't need a television studio to happen.
