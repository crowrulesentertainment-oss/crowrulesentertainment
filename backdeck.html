What Is Back Deck Live?

Back Deck Live is the live companion to the CrowRules Entertainment universe. It's built around direct conversation, community, current projects and the unpredictable energy of a live broadcast.

No giant studio. No corporate network. Just the Back Deck, the cameras and the conversation.
