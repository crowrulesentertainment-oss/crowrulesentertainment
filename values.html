VALUES // HOW WE BUILD

WHAT WE STAND FOR.

CrowRules is being built as more than a collection of entertainment projects. Our values are the operating system behind the company — shaping how we create, collaborate, communicate, grow and make room for other people.

ONE COMPANY. ONE UNIVERSE.

Values become real through action.

A value matters when it changes what we do. From a first idea submitted to Dreamscapes to a production reaching an audience, we want the way we work to be as meaningful as what we make.

THE CROWRULES STANDARD

“Build boldly. Treat people well. Make room for others. Tell the truth about where we are — and keep building.”

01 // OUR CORE

The principles behind the work.

These principles are designed to guide decisions across CrowRules — creative decisions, business decisions, community relationships and the everyday work of building a company from the ground up.

01 / CREATE

CREATIVITY

Original ideas deserve a place to grow. We value imagination, experimentation and the courage to make something that did not exist before.

02 / CONNECT

COMMUNITY

Entertainment can bring people together. We want CrowRules to create opportunities for participation, collaboration and connection.

03 / RESPECT

RESPECT

Creators, audiences, partners, volunteers and teammates deserve dignity. We aim to build relationships on professionalism, empathy and mutual respect.

04 / OPEN

ACCESS

Traditional entertainment pathways can be difficult to enter. We want to create more doors for people with ideas, talent, curiosity and determination.

05 / TRUST

TRANSPARENCY

Clear communication builds trust. We aim to be honest about opportunities, expectations, project status, resources and what we can realistically deliver.

06 / BUILD

THE LONG VIEW

We are building infrastructure, not just individual projects. Today's work should help create possibilities for the next project, creator and generation.

07 / INCLUDE

PEOPLE FIRST

Projects are made by people. We want the human beings behind the work to be seen, heard, credited and treated as part of the story.

08 / LEARN

KEEP GROWING

No company starts with every answer. We value learning, feedback, iteration and the willingness to improve what we build.

09 / BUILD HERE

TACOMA ROOTS

CrowRules is being built from Tacoma. We want that beginning to remain connected to local creators, businesses, audiences and community opportunities.

02 // IN PRACTICE

What our values look like.

Values are easiest to understand when they show up in everyday decisions.

WITH CREATORS

Listen first. Clarify expectations. Protect creative contributions. Give credit. Build pathways for ideas to move forward.

WITH MEMBERS

Make the experience useful, welcoming and understandable. Communicate what membership provides and keep improving the member experience.

WITH PARTNERS

Look for shared value. Be clear about goals, responsibilities and expectations. Build relationships that can grow beyond a single transaction.

WITH OUR COMMUNITY

Show up with humility. Support local opportunities where we can. Listen to feedback and recognize that a company is part of a larger community.

WITH OUR AUDIENCE

Respect people's time and attention. Make experiences worth returning to. Give audiences opportunities to discover, participate and belong.

WITH OURSELVES

Start where we are. Work within our resources. Learn from mistakes. Celebrate progress without pretending the work is finished.

03 // THE COMMITMENT

BUILD SOMETHING
WORTH BELIEVING IN.

CrowRules will evolve. Our projects will change. New people will join. New ideas will reshape the universe. The values give that growth a foundation — creativity with responsibility, ambition with respect, and a long-term vision grounded in real people and real communities.
