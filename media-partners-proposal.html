PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL

THE OPPORTUNITY

CrowRules Entertainment is an independent entertainment company based in Tacoma, Washington. CrowRules Podcasting is being developed to support independent podcast creators and listeners. Tacoma Nights is a CrowRules program focused on Tacoma, Pierce County, the South Sound and wider regional conversations.

We propose a practical exchange: the media partner considers providing a limited amount of complimentary introductory advertising inventory for CrowRules Podcasting, while CrowRules provides agreed promotional recognition and placements for the media partner through its website and Tacoma Nights.

PROPOSED RECIPROCAL VALUE

CROWRULES PROVIDES

  • A dedicated media-partner profile or placement on the CrowRules website during the agreed term.
  • Approved links to the partner's website or selected content.
  • Recurring, clearly identified promotional mentions on Tacoma Nights at a mutually agreed frequency.
  • Additional digital or social cross-promotion only where approved by both parties.
  • A short end-of-pilot recap of delivered placements and available metrics.

MEDIA PARTNER IS INVITED TO PROVIDE

  • Complimentary introductory advertising inventory promoting CrowRules Podcasting.
  • Digital, streaming, radio or television placements selected from inventory the partner chooses to make available.
  • Creative specifications, placement dates and delivery confirmation.
  • A designated contact for coordination and approvals.
  • No cash payment is requested during the proposed pilot.

SUGGESTED PILOT STRUCTURE

Duration: 90 days from the mutually agreed campaign start date.

Compensation: No money exchanged during the pilot. Each party contributes only the promotional inventory and placements expressly agreed in writing.

Deliverables: Exact number, format, placement, timing, creative, frequency and duration to be mutually documented before launch.

Measurement: Review completed placements, available impressions, referrals, clicks, mentions and qualitative feedback. Neither party guarantees audience size, traffic, conversions or business results.

Approvals: Each party retains approval over its own name, logo, marks, copy and creative. No implied endorsement or use of marks outside the written permission.

Review and exit: Review near the end of the pilot. Either party may decline renewal. Cancellation, make-goods, content removal and early termination terms should be confirmed in the written agreement.

IMPORTANT FIT FOR PUBLIC MEDIA

For public television, municipal channels or other organizations that cannot provide commercial advertising, CrowRules welcomes discussion of permitted alternatives such as community calendars, local programming submissions, creator features, public-service announcements or event collaboration. No such placement is assumed or requested outside the organization's policies.
