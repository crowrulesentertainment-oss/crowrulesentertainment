The role

Build the foundation for everything.

The technical lead helps define how the CrowRules websites are structured, how shared services connect, and how the platform can grow without unnecessary duplication.

What this means right now

Flexible, optional involvement—not a promise of a job and not an expectation to work for free. You can simply meet, ask questions, share feedback, or discuss a potential future role. Any activity beyond a conversation must be mutually agreed and appropriately reviewed first.

Potential responsibilities

Plan responsive websites and reusable components; establish GitHub workflows, deployment and testing standards; review existing code; document technical decisions; coordinate Supabase integrations; prioritize the first production-ready properties.

Skills that matter

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git/GitHub, responsive design, API integration, web hosting, accessibility, performance, and the ability to explain trade-offs. Supabase and multi-site architecture are valuable.

Potential first milestone after funding

Audit existing sites and repositories, map the current stack, identify risks, propose shared navigation and components, and produce a prioritized roadmap with estimated effort and costs.

Who we're hoping to meet

Someone pragmatic, collaborative, and comfortable helping a small organization make sound technical decisions. A strong portfolio and clear communication matter more than knowing every tool.
