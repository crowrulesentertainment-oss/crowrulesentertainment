SUNDAY–FRIDAY PRODUCTION RHYTHM

SUNDAYChoose topic, title, angle, guests and segments.
MONDAYResearch, verify facts, gather local stories and prepare interview questions.
TUESDAYWrite host script, segment outlines, transitions and closing.
WEDNESDAYRecord host sections and contributor segments; check audio and video.
THURSDAYEdit, review, approve, prepare thumbnail and YouTube Premiere.
FRIDAYPremiere at 10 PM Pacific, monitor chat and collect viewer feedback.
Editorial guidelines: keep one main topic at the center; distinguish verified facts from opinion; represent people respectfully; avoid presenting a guest's view as the show's official position; invite community suggestions for future episodes.
