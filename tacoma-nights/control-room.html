PRODUCTION PRINCIPLE

One stream. Three scenes. One complete show.

Keep the YouTube livestream running while switching OBS scenes. Do not start a separate Premiere for the episode file.

SCENE 01 · LIVELive Opening

Camera + microphone. Welcome viewers, set up the topic and invite chat participation.

Target: 5–8 minutes

SCENE 02 · MEDIA SOURCERecorded Episode

Transition bumper, then play the final MP4 full-screen with its own audio. Keep the stream active.

Target: Full episode runtime

SCENE 03 · LIVELive Return & Sign-off

Return to camera and microphone. Discuss the episode, read comments, preview next week and close with “Until Next Friday.”

Target: 7–15 minutes
