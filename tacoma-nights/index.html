Remote co-host workspace

Record your segment. Send it to the edit desk.

Co-hosts can securely upload a recorded segment, and leave a note for the edit. Jonathan can review and download submissions for the final Tacoma Nights episode.

Co-host clip drop

Please record in horizontal (16:9) landscape format. Use clear audio, leave a few seconds of silence at the beginning and end, and name your segment so it's easy to identify.

Simple handoff: your name, your clip, and optional notes. Files are stored in a private bucket and are not displayed publicly. Please only submit material you have permission to share.

Accepted formats: MP4, MOV, WebM. Maximum 50 GB per clip (the current resumable-upload limit). Large files use resumable upload so an interrupted connection can retry. Record in horizontal 16:9 landscape where possible.

Add your name and select a video. No sign-in required.

Production handoff

What happens next?

  • 01
    Record horizontallyLandscape 16:9, stable camera, good light, clean audio.
  • 02
    Upload privatelySign in with your approved email and add your segment notes.
  • 03
    Jonathan reviewsThe clip is stored privately for review and download by the production owner.
  • 04
    Edited into the showSelected clips may be trimmed, mixed, captioned, and included in a future episode.

Production owner access

Only the production owner needs to sign in to review and download clips. Co-hosts do not sign in.
