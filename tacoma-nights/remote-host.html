How remote hosting would work.

Remote hosts contribute from home using a computer or phone, a stable internet connection, and a quiet place to record or join a live conversation. The production team coordinates the topic, timing, and format ahead of each appearance.

01 / CONNECT

Meet the production team

After an application review, selected applicants are contacted for an introductory conversation. We'll discuss interests, availability, experience, and the kind of role that could fit.

02 / PREPARE

Plan your segment

You'll receive the episode topic, segment purpose, schedule, and any talking points in advance. Hosts can suggest ideas and help develop their own recurring features.

03 / RECORD

Join remotely

Depending on the episode, you may join a live remote recording or record a segment locally and securely send the video to the edit desk.

04 / REVIEW

Production and editing

Recorded contributions are reviewed by the production owner. Selected material may be edited, mixed, captioned, and scheduled for an episode.

05 / PUBLISH

Be part of the show

Your segment may appear in a Tacoma Nights episode or related show content. Publication is coordinated by the production team and is not guaranteed for every submission.

06 / GROW

Build with the community

As the show develops, remote hosts may help shape recurring segments, interview guests, discuss local topics, and connect viewers with stories across the PNW.

Your setup

You don't need a broadcast studio.

Start with a dependable, comfortable setup. Good sound and a steady connection matter more than expensive equipment. We'll help clarify the technical approach before your first contribution.

  • Computer, laptop, or capable smartphone
  • Reliable broadband internet
  • Headphones or a headset to reduce echo
  • Microphone with clear, understandable audio
  • Quiet space and a simple, stable camera position
  • Landscape video (16:9) for recorded segments whenever possible
  • Willingness to prepare, communicate, and collaborate

Specific software and connection instructions will be provided if you are invited to move forward. No paid equipment purchase is required as a condition of applying.

Who we're looking for

Bring curiosity, reliability, and your own voice.

You don't need to be a professional broadcaster. We're interested in people who can have thoughtful conversations, listen to others, and bring a perspective that adds something meaningful to the show.

Conversation-minded

Comfortable sharing ideas, asking questions, listening, and respectfully disagreeing.

Community-aware

Interested in Tacoma, the South Sound, the Pacific Northwest, or topics that matter to everyday people.

Dependable

Able to communicate about scheduling, prepare for a segment, and let the team know when plans change.

Important opportunity details

Tacoma Nights is an independent production being built from the ground up by CrowRules Entertainment. We're starting at the ground level, building a new entertainment company, developing our programming, and working toward creating something that can grow into a much larger entertainment network.

I'm currently building CrowRules Entertainment without an incoming source of income or dedicated financial backing. Alongside developing this company, I also serve as a caregiver for my elderly mother and my brother, who has significant autism-related support needs. I'm personally investing my time, energy, and effort into making this vision a reality.

At this stage, remote host participation is a volunteer/community collaboration opportunity. CrowRules Entertainment is not currently in a financial position to offer salaries, stipends, or other compensation.

However, future paid positions are not out of the question. As CrowRules Entertainment grows, develops its audience, expands its programming, and reaches a sustainable financial position, the possibility of creating paid opportunities for contributors and production team members is something I absolutely want to explore.

While future compensation may become possible, it is not guaranteed or promised. No salary, stipend, revenue share, future payment, or guaranteed on-air placement is being offered at this time.

Applicants should not purchase equipment or incur expenses expecting reimbursement. Before anyone begins participating, specific responsibilities, expectations, permissions, scheduling, and participation terms will be discussed.

The vision is to build something together today that could create real opportunities tomorrow.
