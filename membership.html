GOOD TO KNOW

MEMBERSHIP FAQ

Can I start for free?

Yes. Choose the free CROW option to create an account. Paid plans are optional and appear only when published by the membership service.

How does paid membership work?

Paid plans use a server-created Stripe Checkout session. Confirm the plan and total shown by Stripe before completing payment. Membership updates may take a short time to appear while payment events are processed.

Where do my CrowPoints and access come from?

The dashboard displays information returned by CrowRules membership services. Availability and benefits depend on the connected project and your current membership.

Can I manage or cancel a paid plan?

When billing management is available for your account, use Manage Billing in the signed-in dashboard to open the hosted billing portal.

What if my account does not load?

Use Refresh in your dashboard or reload this page. If the problem continues, contact CrowRules through the official Contact page and include the error message—never send your password.
