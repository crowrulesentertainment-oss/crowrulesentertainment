Membership Help

FAQ

Simple answers for the universal CrowRules membership system.

Is CROW free?

Yes. CROW is the free CrowRules membership level and does not require a payment method.

How are paid memberships billed?

CROWNER, CREATOR and FOUNDER use monthly Stripe subscriptions. Checkout and billing management are handled by Stripe.

Does one membership work across sites?

Yes. The goal of the universal membership system is one account with shared membership status and site-specific entitlements.

Can I cancel?

Paid members can use the Stripe Customer Portal to manage payment methods, upgrades, downgrades and cancellation settings.

When does my paid membership activate?

After successful Stripe Checkout, Stripe sends the billing event to CrowRules. The membership record is then synchronized into Supabase.

Will I be charged for CROW?

No. CROW is the free tier and remains outside Stripe billing.
