How the universal account works

Create one CrowRules account and your identity is recognized across the network. You do not need a separate membership for every CrowRules site.

How CrowPoints work

Your membership tier determines your CrowPoints multiplier. CROW earns at 1×, CROWNER at 2×, CREATOR at 3× and FOUNDER at 5×.

Upgrading

Paid memberships use Stripe's secure hosted Checkout. After payment, the CrowRules membership system receives the subscription status and updates your universal account.

Managing your membership

Paid members can use the Stripe Customer Portal to manage billing, payment methods, subscription changes and cancellation.
