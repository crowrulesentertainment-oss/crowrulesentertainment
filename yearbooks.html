02 / The Platform

More Than A
Printed Yearbook.

The goal is to turn a school year into a living digital memory archive that can combine traditional yearbook content with modern video and interactive media.

01 / VIDEO

School Video Yearbooks

A future video edition could bring the entire school year to life through interviews, performances, events and student-created stories.

02 / DIGITAL

Interactive Yearbooks

Digital pages could connect photographs, stories, videos, galleries and school memories into one interactive experience.

03 / CLASS

Class Yearbooks

Future editions could support individual classes, grade levels, graduating classes and special class projects.

04 / SENIOR

Senior & Graduation

Preserve the final year through senior memories, graduation, messages, activities and milestone moments.

05 / SPORTS

Sports & Athletics

A future edition could include teams, seasons, championships, tournaments, coaches, photos and highlight videos.

06 / CLUBS

Clubs & Organizations

Capture the groups, organizations, student leadership and activities that make each school unique.

07 / PEOPLE

Student & Staff Spotlights

Future profiles could celebrate students, teachers, staff, coaches and other members of the school community.

08 / ARCHIVE

School Memory Archive

Preserve school traditions, milestones, photographs, videos and important moments beyond a single printed book.
