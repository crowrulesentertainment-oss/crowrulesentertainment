How We Build

From Idea
To Screen.

Every production begins with an idea. The studio process turns that idea into something an audience can experience.

01

DEVELOPMENT

Concepts, formats, characters, stories and production plans are developed into a workable project.

02

PRE-PRODUCTION

Planning, scheduling, locations, equipment, crew and creative preparation happen before production begins.

03

PRODUCTION

The cameras roll. Interviews happen. Scenes are captured. The idea becomes real footage.

04

POST-PRODUCTION

Editing, sound, graphics, color, music and finishing turn raw material into finished entertainment.
