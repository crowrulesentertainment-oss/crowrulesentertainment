Working At CrowRules

This Isn't
Corporate Media.

CrowRules is designed around independence, experimentation and people who want to make things. The goal isn't to recreate an old media company. It's to build something different.

01 / CREATE

Make Things
Worth Watching.

Shows, films, broadcasts, podcasts, music, digital projects and ideas that have an actual reason to exist.

02 / BUILD

Build The
Infrastructure.

Media doesn't happen without people building the systems behind it — technology, production, operations, design and community.

03 / CONNECT

Put People
First.

Creators, viewers, members, partners and audiences are not numbers. They are the reason the network exists.

04 / EXPERIMENT

Try The
Weird Idea.

Not every experiment will work. That's okay. Independent media needs room to test ideas without waiting for permission.

The CrowRules Code

Why
CrowRules?

A job should be more than a title. It should be a chance to participate in something you actually care about.

01

Independent Thinking

Bring your perspective. Question assumptions. Good ideas don't need permission to be useful.

02

Ownership

Take responsibility for what you build and help shape the systems around you.

03

Creative Freedom

Experiment with formats, ideas and ways of connecting audiences with stories.

04

Cross-Discipline Work

Television can connect to music. Technology can connect to storytelling. Events can connect to community.

05

Build From The Ground Up

You're not just maintaining an established system. You're helping define what the system becomes.

06

People Matter

Respect, communication, curiosity and collaboration matter as much as technical ability.
