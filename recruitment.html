Important note about unpaid participation

CrowRules Entertainment is being developed as a company. Calling someone a volunteer does not automatically make unpaid work lawful for a for-profit business. This page is for expressions of interest and conversations only. Before inviting anyone to perform productive work, the company will need to review the arrangement with qualified employment counsel and comply with applicable federal and Washington requirements. Applicants should not provide services, create deliverables, spend money, or rely on promised future compensation unless a lawful written arrangement is in place.
