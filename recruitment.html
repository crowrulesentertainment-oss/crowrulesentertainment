Ways to help right now

Start small. Keep it voluntary.

You do not need to join as a full-time teammate. Tell us what you might enjoy, and we can explore a clearly bounded next step together.

Share ideas and feedback

Review a concept, test a page as a visitor, suggest improvements, or join a short brainstorming conversation. No ongoing commitment required.

Learn and collaborate

Explore public learning sessions, portfolio discussions, documentation ideas, or community conversations where expectations are agreed in advance.

Help shape the roadmap

Tell us which division interests you and what a realistic future contribution might look like. Any hands-on activity must have an agreed scope and an appropriate, lawful arrangement first.

Our promise to prospective volunteers

We will be honest about funding, avoid pressure or guilt, respect your time, and not imply that unpaid participation guarantees a job, ownership, credit, or future compensation. You can decline or stop at any time. We will confirm expectations before any activity begins.
