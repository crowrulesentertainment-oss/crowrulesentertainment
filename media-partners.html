CrowRules Media Partners is a proposed mutual-promotion initiative connecting CrowRules Entertainment with television, radio, streaming and digital media organizations across Tacoma, the South Sound and the Pacific Northwest.

01 // THE IDEA

A TWO-WAY PROMOTIONAL RELATIONSHIP.

We are not asking media organizations to provide advertising without value in return. We are proposing a clearly defined exchange of approved promotional placements, with deliverables agreed by both parties.

01 / INTRODUCE

REACH NEW AUDIENCES

Introduce CrowRules Podcasting to audiences who may be interested in discovering independent shows, creators and new listening experiences.

02 / RECIPROCATE

PROMOTE OUR MEDIA PARTNER

Provide agreed partner recognition, links and promotional mentions through CrowRules Podcasting and Tacoma Nights.

03 / MEASURE

LEARN AND IMPROVE

Review the campaign together using available referral, click, placement and engagement information—without promising audience numbers in advance.

02 // THE EXCHANGE

VALUE ON BOTH SIDES.

The initial request is for complimentary introductory advertising inventory, in exchange for promotional exposure that CrowRules can provide.

WHAT CROWRULES OFFERS

  • Dedicated media-partner recognition and a link on the CrowRules website, during the agreed term.
  • Recurring, clearly identified promotional mentions on Tacoma Nights, at a frequency agreed in writing.
  • Approved partner links or promotional creative within relevant CrowRules Podcasting pages or placements.
  • Optional social or event cross-promotion when both parties approve the content and schedule.
  • A short campaign recap using available, supportable performance data.

WHAT WE ARE REQUESTING

  • Complimentary introductory advertising placements for CrowRules Podcasting, subject to inventory and approval.
  • Consideration for digital display, streaming, connected-TV, radio or television inventory, depending on the partner's channels.
  • Agreed placement details, timing, creative specifications and campaign reporting where available.
  • A designated contact for coordinating creative approvals and campaign delivery.
  • No payment or cash sponsorship during the proposed introductory pilot.

06 // QUESTIONS

PARTNERSHIP FAQ.

Is CrowRules asking for free advertising?

Yes. The initial proposal requests complimentary introductory advertising inventory in exchange for specific, approved promotional exposure through CrowRules. It is a proposed barter arrangement, not a request for an unconditional donation.

Does CrowRules guarantee a certain audience size?

No. CrowRules will not promise impressions, listeners, viewers, clicks or conversions that it cannot substantiate. The parties can agree on measurable deliverables and review available results.

Is the media organization already a partner?

No. All organizations shown on this page are prospects unless and until both parties approve and sign or otherwise confirm an agreement.

Can the partner choose different terms?

Yes. The 90-day pilot is a starting proposal. Inventory, schedule, term, creative, reporting and reciprocal placements can be negotiated.

What if a broadcaster cannot provide free commercial advertising?

We can ask about permitted alternatives such as community calendars, public-service announcements, creator features, local programming submissions or event collaboration. These are not assumed to be available.

How do we start?

Contact CrowRules with the organization name, the appropriate advertising or partnership contact, and the promotional channels you would like to discuss.
