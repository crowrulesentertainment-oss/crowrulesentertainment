Record a campaign report

Enter figures from your campaign platform or sponsor report. Clicks and conversions are reported figures unless you identify a verified tracking source. Campaign spend and attributed revenue must use the same currency for ROI to be meaningful.

How ROI is calculated

Cost per clickCampaign spend ÷ clicks
Cost per conversionCampaign spend ÷ conversions
Return on ad spendAttributed revenue ÷ spend
ROI(Revenue − spend) ÷ spend

Calculations are shown only when a non-zero spend and compatible currency are recorded. An ROI result is an estimate based on the values entered, not an independently verified sales audit. No CPM is shown because the system does not yet measure verified ad impressions.
