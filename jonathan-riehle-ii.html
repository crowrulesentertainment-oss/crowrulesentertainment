Jonathan Riehle II is the founder and owner of CrowRules Entertainment, an independent entertainment company focused on building a connected universe for stories, creators, communities, and original media. As CEO, he guides the company’s vision, creative direction, partnerships, and development of its growing digital platforms.

THE VISION

CrowRules Entertainment is being built from the ground up with the goal of connecting different entertainment experiences under one company. The guiding idea is simple: give creators room to develop, help stories find an audience, and build a community around shared creativity.

One company. One universe.

AREAS OF FOCUS

  • Company strategy and development
  • Creator community and podcasting
  • Original stories and creative worlds
  • Digital media and entertainment platforms
  • Community partnerships and collaboration

BUILDING IN PUBLIC

Jonathan shares the process of building CrowRules, including the challenges, lessons, experiments, and milestones involved in turning a long-term vision into a working independent media company.

The approach is practical: start small, listen to creators, test ideas, and keep improving.
