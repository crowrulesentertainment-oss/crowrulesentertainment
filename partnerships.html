06 // QUESTIONS

PARTNERSHIP FAQ.

Do we need to commit to a large sponsorship?

No. CrowRules is open to conversations ranging from early support and project sponsorship to creative collaboration, resources, mentorship and longer-term partnerships.

Can a local Tacoma business participate?

Yes. Local businesses can explore sponsorship, community partnership, advertising, events, production support and other forms of collaboration.

Can a partner support one specific project?

Yes. Project-based sponsorship can be structured around a show, podcast, event, production, campaign or other defined initiative.

Can partnerships include mentorship or expertise?

Yes. Financial support is not the only possible contribution. Knowledge, introductions, services, equipment, locations and professional expertise may also be valuable.

Are partnership benefits guaranteed?

Specific recognition, promotional placements, deliverables and other benefits should be agreed upon for each partnership rather than assumed.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

Your inquiry is routed to the CrowRules partnership team for review. The next step can be a conversation about your goals, timing and the type of collaboration you have in mind.
