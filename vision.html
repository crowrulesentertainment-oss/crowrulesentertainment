03 // THE ROADMAP

BUILDING IN CHAPTERS.

The roadmap is a direction, not a promise that every milestone will arrive on an exact date. The goal is sustainable growth from a small foundation.

2026

FOUNDATION

Core infrastructure, identity, membership, community relationships and the systems needed to build the universe.

2027

SPORTS + PODCASTING

Expand into sports and audio while continuing to develop the creator and membership ecosystem.

2028

TV + COMMUNITY

Grow CrowRules TV and develop the next generation of community experiences around CrowSpace.

2029

Expand the legacy and recognition side of CrowRules through yearbooks and the Spectrum Awards.

2031

RECORDS + STUDIOS

Build toward larger production capabilities and additional creative infrastructure as resources and partnerships allow.
