Recruitment privacy

Your information deserves care.

This notice describes how CrowRules Entertainment intends to handle information submitted through its recruitment interest form.

Information collected

The form may collect your name, email address, role of interest, skills and experience, portfolio or professional link, preferred location, optional message, and the date you consented to recruitment-related contact.

Why we use it

Information is intended to be used to review expressions of interest, contact candidates about recruitment-related conversations, assess potential role alignment, and maintain a recruitment pipeline.

Who can access it

Access should be limited to authorized CrowRules recruitment personnel. The application service uses Supabase infrastructure. Do not submit passwords, financial information, government identification, confidential employer information, or other sensitive personal data.

Retention and deletion requests

Recruitment information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated recruitment purpose and applicable recordkeeping obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion, use a verified official CrowRules contact channel once published. Do not assume deletion is complete until you receive confirmation.

Security and third-party services

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained, including access controls and restricted administrator access. Internet transmission cannot be guaranteed completely secure. The form service may be unavailable from time to time.

Funding status and recruitment

Submitting interest does not create an employment relationship or guarantee an interview, job, compensation, or future opening. Current founding-team opportunities are funding-pending. Productive work should not begin until a lawful, written arrangement is in place.

Contact and updates

A verified recruitment contact email and the date this notice takes effect should be added before public launch. This notice is a draft operational privacy notice, not legal advice; review it against CrowRules' actual practices and applicable laws before publishing.
